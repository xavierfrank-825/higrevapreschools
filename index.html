A small, bright school in the heart of Marathahalli

HIGREVA Preschool welcomes children from 18 months to 6 years. Our classrooms are painted with animals, trees and flowers, and children learn in small circles at child-sized tables, with teachers sitting right beside them.

Every day mixes books, colouring, rhymes and play. Through the Heureka curriculum we help children notice, ask and share their thinking, so they grow in confidence long before big school.

Heureka: learning that makes thinking visible

Children don’t only get answers. They wonder, test ideas, talk about what they notice and see their own thinking grow. Special days, like our space dome visit, turn big ideas into things they can step inside.
